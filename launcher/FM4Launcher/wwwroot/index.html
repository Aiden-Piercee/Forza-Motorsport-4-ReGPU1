About FM4:ReGPU

A native PC port of Forza Motorsport 4 (Xbox 360). The game's PowerPC code is statically recompiled to x64, it runs on our own implementation of the Xbox 360 kernel, and a native Direct3D 12 renderer replaces the console's GPU.

No game data is included: it plays from your own copy of the disc.
